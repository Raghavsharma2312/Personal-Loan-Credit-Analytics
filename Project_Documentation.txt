PERSONAL LOAN & CREDIT RISK ANALYTICS
=====================================

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
-------------------

This project analyzes personal loan applications, loan disbursements,
customer credit profiles, and payment behavior for a financial institution.

The objective is to identify loan portfolio trends, customer patterns,
repayment behavior, and potential credit risk.


2. BUSINESS OBJECTIVES
----------------------

• Analyze customer profiles and credit scores.
• Analyze loan applications and approval/rejection trends.
• Analyze loan disbursement and loan portfolio.
• Analyze payment and repayment behavior.
• Identify default and high-risk customers.
• Compare loan performance across branches and customer segments.


3. DATABASE TABLES
------------------

Customers
- Stores customer demographic, income, employment and credit information.

Branches
- Stores branch and regional information.

LoanApplications
- Stores customer loan applications and application status.

Loans
- Stores approved/disbursed loan information and loan status.

Payments
- Stores loan payment, due amount, paid amount and payment status.

5. BUSINESS ANALYSIS AREAS
--------------------------

Customer Analysis:
- Customer count
- Average income
- Credit score analysis
- Employment type analysis

Loan Application Analysis:
- Total applications
- Approved applications
- Rejected applications
- Approval rate
- Rejection rate

Loan Portfolio Analysis:
- Total loan amount
- Average loan amount
- Loan status analysis
- Branch-wise loan analysis
- Customer-wise loan analysis

Payment Analysis:
- Total amount due
- Total amount paid
- Outstanding amount
- Late payment analysis
- Payment delay analysis

Credit Risk Analysis:
- Default loan analysis
- Default percentage
- Default rate by employment type
- Low credit score customers with default loans
- High-risk loan identification
